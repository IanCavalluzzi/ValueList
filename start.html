Value Statuses

- ↑↑Overpaid: The best value status a unit can have. This means the tower is frequently getting offers above its base value.
- ↑Increasing: This means the tower is most likely going to increase in value.
- ↻Improving: This means the tower is recovering after dropping or after a long period of stability.
- =Stabilized: The tower is at a stable value that doesn't get much overpay or underpay.
- ↭Fluctuating: The tower gets both underpayment and overpayment, represented with a range of values instead of a set value.
- ↷Declining: The tower is slowly losing value, but may stabilize soon.
- ↓Decreasing: This means the tower is most likely going to decrease in value.
- ↓↓Underpaid: This means the unit is frequently getting offers below its base value.

- Offsale: The item cannot be obtainable through any method other than Trading.
- Exclusive: The item will go Offsale within the near future.
